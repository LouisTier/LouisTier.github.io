Robust and Reliable Data-Driven Framework for Quality-Related Knowledge Discovery in Large-Scale Rubber Mixing Batch Processes

L. Berthier, A. Shokry, E. Moulines, S. Desroziers, G. Ramelet

To be determined
Submitted to Journal of Cheminformatics

This work introduces an explainable and trustworthy machine learning framework for quality prediction in rubber mixing, combining XGBoost, explainability, and uncertainty quantification. The approach uncovers key process insights, reduces input complexity by 82%, and achieves high predictive accuracy — validated on real-world Michelin data.

Detecting fast-ripples on both micro- and macro-electrodes in epilepsy: a Fourier Analysis-based Scalogram based detector (FAST)

L. Gardy*, E. J. Barbeau, J. Curot, L. Valton C. Hurter, L. Berthier

March 2025
Journal of Neuroscience Methods

We present a novel approach for detecting fast-ripples in epilepsy patients using Fourier Analysis-based Scalogram techniques, potentially improving the diagnosis and treatment of epilepsy.
