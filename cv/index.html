Experience

Augmodo · Seattle, WA, USA (remote) · since August 2025

Real-time Spatial AI for retail: open-world computer vision, with Sacha Hu, Ashley Napier, and Galen Han.

  • Developed open-world VLMs that recognize products, aisles, and price tags from images alone, trained on paired images and product descriptions for identification and planogram matching.
  • Used test-time augmentation to estimate the confidence of open-world classifiers (product identification, QC automation) and flag the uncertain predictions for human QC, across 100k+ images/day.
  • Fine-tuned YOLO and the transformer-based RF-DETR with hard-negative mining and multi-GPU cloud training, to suppress confirmed empty-shelf false positives without degrading other shelf classes.
  • Built the RGB+depth version of a classifier that checks empty-shelf alerts, with depth estimated by Depth Anything V2 as a fourth input channel, to tell true gaps from dark or shadowed shelves.
  • Designed automated relabeling pipelines that update legacy bounding-box annotations at scale, adapting large datasets to newer architectures without manual re-annotation.

Michelin · Clermont-Ferrand, France · March 2024 to February 2027

Conformal and adaptive soft sensors for real-time quality prediction in industrial batch processes. Supervised by Guillaume Ramelet and Dr. Maxime Moreaud.

  • Introduced Self-Organized Conformal Prediction (NeurIPS 2026): unsupervised group discovery with a Self-Organizing Map for group-local calibration, cutting regional coverage gaps on all 10 datasets, by 14.8% on average, for only 3% larger prediction sets.
  • Co-developed Mask-Valid Conformal Prediction (with M. Hegazy, M. I. Jordan, A. Dieuleveut): binary monitoring masks that interpolate between marginal and anytime coverage, with restart validity after failures and no need for past test labels.
  • Created and open-sourced TorchSOM (JMLR 2026), a PyTorch library for Self-Organizing Maps: 81-98% faster than MiniSom on CPU, up to about 12× faster than somoclu on GPU, and the lowest topographic error in every rectangular-map benchmark (62-100% below MiniSom).
  • Developed adaptive Just-in-Time soft sensors pairing SOM similarity retrieval with online SHAP feature selection, cutting MSE by up to 61% over global baselines (IEEE ETFA 2026) and prediction error by over 90% under year-long drift on 35k production batches.
  • Built an explainable analytics framework (frequency-ranked feature selection, SHAP interactions, multi-split conformal prediction) on 35k rubber-mixing records, cutting 316 input variables to 20 at equal accuracy (Digit. Chem. Eng. 2026).

ENSTA Paris · Paris, France · September 2024 to October 2026

Led the lab sessions of Introduction to Machine Learning and Data Science for first-year engineering students (B3/MSc1): 20h a year for three consecutive years, 20 to 25 students per session. Supervised by Pr. Philippe Xu.

  • Taught the fundamentals of machine learning, from data preprocessing and feature engineering to model training and evaluation.
  • Guided students through their first hands-on experience building and validating predictive models.

AIRL, Imperial College London · London, UK · April 2023 to September 2023

Model-based uncertainty quantification for sample-efficient quality-diversity optimization in robotics. Supervised by Pr. Antoine Cully.

  • Tackled the data-hunger of MAP-Elites by integrating probabilistic surrogate models on a robotic arm control task; the best variant beat MAP-Elites and came close to MAP-Elites with sampling.
  • Benchmarked eight algorithmic variants combining different surrogate architectures and acquisition strategies in non-deterministic environments.
  • Introduced two metrics for the uncertainty of quality-diversity archives.

CNRS (CerCo) · Toulouse, France · May 2022 to July 2022

Deep learning for detecting fast ripples, pathological biomarkers for epileptic tissue, in intracranial EEG signals. Supervised by Dr. Ludovic Gardy, Pr. Christophe Hurter, and Pr. Emmanuel Barbeau.

  • Trained CNN fast-ripple detectors on wavelet time-frequency images of macro- and micro-electrode EEG, improving accuracy by 5% through systematic hyperparameter tuning (J. Neurosci. Methods 2025).
  • Applied Grad-CAM to localize high-frequency bursts in time-frequency maps, giving clinicians visual explanations anchored to known fast-ripple signatures.
  • Accelerated EEG signal and image processing by 15% with Cython, and built 7 NoSQL databases for multi-channel EEG at macro and micro electrode scales for batch retrieval and parallel processing.

EuroMov DHM · Alès, France · January 2022 to April 2022

Anisotropic image filtering for sub-pixel accurate line feature detection, published at IEEE ICASSP 2023. Supervised by Pr. Baptiste Magnier and Pr. Binbin Xu.

  • Designed the 2D Semi Bi-Gaussian (2DSBG) filter, an asymmetric convolution kernel that enhances elongated structures while suppressing isotropic noise.
  • Benchmarked against state-of-the-art edge detection models (DexiNed, RCF, Laplacian-of-Gaussian) on synthetic and real-world datasets.
