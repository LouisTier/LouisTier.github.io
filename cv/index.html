Work

  • 2025.08 - NOW
    Computer Vision Scientist
    Augmodo
    Uncertainty-aware computer vision for open-world retail deployments, processing millions of images daily.
    • Working alongside Sacha Hu (Senior CV Engineer), Ashley Napier (CV Group Lead), and Shubham Wagh (Senior CV Engineer).
    • Building uncertainty-aware classifiers for product identification with test-time augmentation, reducing manual QC workload by selectively accepting high-confidence predictions.
    • Developing an RGB+depth pipeline to flag empty shelf areas, focusing human review on genuinely uncertain cases.
    • Designing low-cost dataset relabeling strategies to update legacy bounding box annotations at scale using improved detectors.
    • Fine-tuning object detectors (YOLO and the transformer-based RF-DETR) for empty shelf-area detection, leveraging hard-negative examples to push detection performance; handling training and fine-tuning on cloud GPUs.
  • 2024.03 - NOW
    Research Engineer (PhD Contract)
    Michelin
    Adaptive soft sensing and conformal prediction for real-time quality monitoring in rubber manufacturing.
    • Supervised by Dr. Sylvain Desroziers, Dr. Maxime Moreaud, and Guillaume Ramelet.
    • Developed TorchSOM, the reference PyTorch library for Self-Organizing Maps, released under Apache 2.0 via Michelin's open-source organization (74-98% faster than MiniSom on CPU, GPU-accelerated, scikit-learn API, 90% test coverage).
    • Designed and benchmarked five adaptive soft sensing strategies for real-time quality monitoring, validated on 35,125 production batches, cutting prediction error by around 59% short-term and over 90% under year-long drift.
    • Developed Self-Organized CP and Masked CP, two conformal prediction frameworks for locally adaptive and mask-valid sequential uncertainty quantification in industrial settings.
  • 2024 - 2025

    Led machine learning lab sessions for first-year engineering students, with 20 to 25 students per session.

  • 2023.04 - 2023.09
    Research Assistant (ML & Optimization)
    Adaptive & Intelligent Robotics Lab (AIRL)
    Sample-efficient Quality-Diversity optimization using surrogate models for robotic control in uncertain environments.
    • Supervised by Pr. Antoine Cully.
    • Tackled the data-hunger of MAP-Elites by integrating probabilistic surrogate models, achieving significant gains in sample efficiency on a robotic arm control task.
    • Benchmarked eight algorithmic variants combining different surrogate architectures and acquisition strategies in non-deterministic environments.
  • 2022.05 - 2022.08
    Research Intern (ML & Neuroscience)
    Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique (CNRS)
    Deep learning for detecting fast ripples, pathological biomarkers for epileptic tissue, in intracranial EEG signals.
    • Supervised by Dr. Ludovic Gardy, Pr. Christophe Hurter, and Pr. Emmanuel Barbeau.
    • Built a CNN pipeline converting raw EEG signals into time-frequency scalograms via continuous wavelet transform for automated fast ripple classification.
    • Applied Grad-CAM to provide clinicians with visual explanations of which signal features drive each prediction.
    • Accelerated the processing pipeline by 15% through Cython optimization; improved classification accuracy by 5% via hyperparameter tuning.
  • 2022.01 - 2022.04
    Research Assistant (ML & CV)
    EuroMoov Digital Health in Motion (DHM)
    Anisotropic image filtering for sub-pixel accurate line feature detection, published at IEEE ICASSP 2023.
    • Supervised by Pr. Baptiste Magnier and Pr. Binbin Xu.
    • Designed the 2D Semi Bi-Gaussian (2DSBG) filter, an asymmetric convolution kernel that enhances elongated structures while suppressing isotropic noise.
    • Benchmarked against state-of-the-art edge detection models (DexiNed, RCF, Laplacian-of-Gaussian) on synthetic and real-world datasets.
