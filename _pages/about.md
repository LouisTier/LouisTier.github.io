I am a PhD candidate at the [Centre de Mathématiques Appliquées (CMAP)](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/) of [École Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/), in the [SIMPAS](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/recherche/decision-et-donnees/simpas) team.
My research is a CIFRE collaboration with [Michelin](https://www.michelin.com/), supervised by [Pr. Eric Moulines](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-moulines-61512860/), [Dr. Ahmed Shokry](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmed-shokry-aa705075/), [Dr. Maxime Moreaud](https://www.linkedin.com/in/moreaudmaxime/), and [Guillaume Ramelet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/guillaume-ramelet-7696081/).

I build machine learning systems that predict tire quality in real time on Michelin's production lines, replacing slow and costly laboratory measurements, and, just as important, that flag when their own prediction should not be trusted.
My work sits at the crossroads of machine learning, uncertainty quantification (especially conformal prediction), and explainability, with the long-term goal of enabling autonomous process control in manufacturing. You can read more on my [research page](/research/).

Beyond my PhD, I work on two other fronts:

- **Spatial AI for retail**: as a Computer Vision Scientist at [Augmodo](https://www.augmodo.com/), I build the vision models behind its real-time Spatial AI, which tracks every product in every store from badge cameras worn by store associates; they recognize products, aisles, and price tags in open-world stores, and use test-time augmentation to estimate how much each prediction can be trusted and flag the uncertain ones for human review, across more than 100,000 images a day
- **World models for EEG**: in the 2026 inaugural cohort of the [Unaite Fellowship for AI in Biology](https://fellowship.unaite.fr/program), mentored by [Dr. Quentin Garrido](https://garridoq.com/) and [Dr. Jean-Baptiste Masson](https://research.pasteur.fr/fr/member/jean-baptiste-masson/), I work on generative and world models for EEG, bringing together brain signals, which I first studied at the CNRS, and world models, which I explored with the [fly-brain world model](/projects/3_hack_the_worlds/) that took second place at Hack the World(s)

Before my PhD, I explored machine learning across several domains:

- **Robotics**: [Adaptive & Intelligent Robotics Lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/adaptive-intelligent-robotics/), Imperial College London, sample-efficient Quality-Diversity optimization with surrogate models
- **Neuroscience**: [CNRS CerCo](https://cerco.cnrs.fr/), deep learning for pathological oscillation detection in epileptic EEG signals
- **Healthcare**: [EuroMov DHM](https://dhm.euromov.eu/), image processing and biomedical signal analysis

## Collaboration

I enjoy bridging academic research and real-world applications. Alongside my PhD, I work with startups and industry partners on applied ML, from agentic systems to computer vision at scale to uncertainty quantification.

If you are working on a problem where machine learning has to be reliable, not just accurate, I would genuinely like to hear about it. That can take many shapes, a research internship, focused consulting, or a joint project with concrete deliverables.

Domains of interest:
- **AI for Science**: Neuroscience, Bio AI, Healthcare
- **Industry**: Manufacturing, Robotics, Supply Chain, Spatial & Open-world AI
- **Commerce**: Retail, Finance

Interested? [Let's talk](mailto:louis.berthier@polytechnique.edu).
