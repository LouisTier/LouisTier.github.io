I am a CIFRE PhD student in *Applied Mathematics & Machine Learning* at [École Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/), affiliated with the [Centre de Mathématiques Appliquées (CMAP)](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/) and the [SIMPAS](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/recherche/decision-et-donnees/simpas) team.  
My research is conducted in collaboration [Michelin](https://www.michelin.com/), under the supervision of [Pr. Eric Moulines](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-moulines-61512860/), [Dr. Ahmed Shokry](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmed-shokry-aa705075/), [Dr. Maxime Moreaud](https://www.linkedin.com/in/moreaudmaxime/), and [Guillaume Ramelet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/guillaume-ramelet-7696081/).

My research focuses on the development of *data-driven soft sensors for real-time quality monitoring* in rubber production lines.  
The objective is to overcome the limitations of expensive and offline quality measurements by building **adaptive predictive systems** that provide **reliable, online estimations of product quality**.  
This involves the integration of **machine learning**, **uncertainty quantification** -notably via conformal prediction-, and **explainability** to ensure robust deployment in complex industrial environments.

Prior to that, my research focused on  **machine/deep learning** and **uncertainty quantification** applied to:

- Robotics - [Adaptive & Intelligent Robotics Lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/adaptive-intelligent-robotics/), Imperial College London: model-based Quality-Diversity optimization
- Neuroscience - [CNRS CerCo](https://cerco.cnrs.fr/): CNN-based oscillation detection in neurophysiological data  
- Healthcare - [EuroMov DHM](https://www.euromov-dhm.eu/): image processing and biomedical signal analysis

## Industrial Collaboration

I am actively engaged in **industrial collaborations** as part of my PhD, having worked with **two startups**, one of which I am **still collaborating with**. My goal is to translate research outcomes into **practical, industry-ready solutions**.

Possible collaboration formats include:

- Targeted **research internships**
- **Part-time collaborations** (20 hours/week)
- **Joint research projects** with defined milestones and deliverables

Domains of interest include **Neuroscience**, **BioAI**, **Retail**, **Marketing**, **Manufacturing**, **Finance**, **Robotics** and **Supply Chain**.

If you are interested in collaboration or wish to discuss potential applications of my research, please [contact me](mailto:louis.berthier@polytechnique.edu).
