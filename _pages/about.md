I am a CIFRE PhD student in Applied Mathematics and Machine Learning at [Ecole Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/), affiliated with the [Centre de Mathematiques Appliquees (CMAP)](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/) and the [SIMPAS](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/recherche/decision-et-donnees/simpas) team.
My research is conducted in collaboration with [Michelin](https://www.michelin.com/), supervised by [Pr. Eric Moulines](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eric-moulines-61512860/), [Dr. Ahmed Shokry](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmed-shokry-aa705075/), [Dr. Maxime Moreaud](https://www.linkedin.com/in/moreaudmaxime/), and [Guillaume Ramelet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/guillaume-ramelet-7696081/).

My work focuses on data-driven soft sensors for real-time quality monitoring in rubber production.
The goal is to replace expensive offline measurements with adaptive predictive systems that provide reliable online quality estimations, combining machine learning, uncertainty quantification (notably conformal prediction), and explainability for robust industrial deployment.

Previously, I worked on machine learning and uncertainty quantification in:

- **Robotics** — [Adaptive & Intelligent Robotics Lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/adaptive-intelligent-robotics/), Imperial College London: model-based Quality-Diversity optimization
- **Neuroscience** — [CNRS CerCo](https://cerco.cnrs.fr/): CNN-based oscillation detection in neurophysiological data
- **Healthcare** — [EuroMov DHM](https://www.euromov-dhm.eu/): image processing and biomedical signal analysis
