## Research

My work focuses on **data-driven soft sensing** for *real-time quality prediction* in multistage industrial processes, particularly in the rubber industry.  
I develop **machine learning methods** integrating **uncertainty quantification** (e.g., conformal prediction) and **explainability** to enable reliable deployment of predictive models in complex manufacturing environments.

## Background

I hold a **double master’s degree**:

- MSc in *Advanced Computing* — [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/)  
- Diplôme d’ingénieur in *AI & Data Science* — [IMT Mines Alès](https://www.imt-mines-ales.fr/)

Previous research experience includes:

- [Adaptive & Intelligent Robotics Lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/adaptive-intelligent-robotics/), Imperial College — model-based Quality-Diversity optimization  
- [CNRS CerCo](https://cerco.cnrs.fr/), Toulouse — CNN-based oscillation detection in neurophysiological data  
- [EuroMov DHM](https://www.euromov-dhm.eu/), IMT Mines Alès — image processing and biomedical signal analysis  

My broader interests include **online sensing**, **uncertainty modeling**, **generative methods**, and **domain adaptation** for real-world systems.
