## Hack the World(s): World Models <span class="badge bg-secondary text-light lb-award"><i class="fa-solid fa-medal me-1"></i> 2nd place</span>
{: #hack-the-worlds-world-models}

<div class="card lb-event mt-3">
    <div class="row no-gutters">
        <div class="col-md-4 lb-event-image">
            <a href="/projects/3_hack_the_worlds/">
                {% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/projects/fly_thumbnail.jpg" alt="Wiring the Fly Brain into a World Model" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" sizes="(min-width: 768px) 280px, 100vw" %}
            </a>
        </div>
        <div class="col-md-8">
            <div class="card-body">
                <p class="card-text">
                    A 24-hour hackathon dedicated to <strong>world models</strong>, the next frontier of AI, held with the support of <strong>Yann LeCun</strong>.
                    One hundred students in twenty-five teams, drawn from France's top engineering schools and universities, built systems around the <strong>JEPA</strong> (Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture) paradigm, with high-performance GPU compute and researchers on hand through the night.
                    The opening conference, featuring Philippe Baptiste and Yann LeCun, was held at PSL's Paris School of AI.
                </p>
                <p class="card-text">
                    With team <strong>Piaget</strong>, I built <strong><a href="/projects/3_hack_the_worlds/">Wiring the Fly Brain into a World Model</a></strong>, a JEPA world model trained on a complete fly-brain connectome (FlyWire, ~140k neurons) embodied in a simulated fly, built region by region on the EB-JEPA framework. <strong>We placed 2nd</strong>, and the project was reposted and congratulated by <strong>Yann LeCun</strong>.
                    We later wrote it up as a <a href="https://hal.science/hal-05674559">working paper on HAL</a> and <a href="/seminars/#seminar-lix-2026">presented it at LIX</a>, École Polytechnique's Computer Science Laboratory.
                    With <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucas-andrei-thil/">Lucas Thil</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/daniel-nowak-assis-77a6311a2/">Daniel Nowak-Assis</a>, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/adam-jlidi-1a9504296/">Adam Jlidi</a>.
                </p>
                <p class="text-muted small mb-3">
                    June 19-20, 2026 · ESIEE Paris, Noisy-le-Grand (opening at PSL, Paris) · Organized by ESIEE Paris, Mines Nancy/ACADI &amp; PR[AI]RIE, with AI Factory France · All participants received VivaTech invitations
                </p>

            </a>
        </div>
        <div class="col-md-8">
            <div class="card-body">
                <p class="card-text">
                    France's premier student hackathon dedicated to <strong>Generative AI and Fintech</strong>: 150 hackers, 24 non-stop hours at HEC Paris.
                    Teams built concrete solutions for the financial sector with access to sponsor AI tooling, mentored by engineers and entrepreneurs, and pitched to a jury of VCs and unicorn founders.
                </p>
                <p class="card-text">
                    With my team, I built <strong><a href="/projects/2_paris_fintech/">WEBI</a></strong>, an AI copilot that turns fragmented bank deal flow into a mandate-matched, negotiation-ready pipeline for structured bond products, powered by Gemini 2.0 Flash and a FAISS vector index. We pitched it to a jury of VCs and founders. No prize this round.
                    With <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/emile-jouannet-1225aa251/">Emile Jouannet</a>, <a href="https://mathildeanitathierry.com/">Mathilde Anita Thierry</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/elie-de-baudus/">Elie de Baudus</a>, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/roland-sosin/">Roland Sosin</a>.
